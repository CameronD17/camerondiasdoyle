## Running & Fitness

My routine for now feels very solid, I have a weekly plan that I stick to fairly religiously and I can definitely see the improvements. Having a structure like this helps me massively in the way I approach things. It's along the lines of Steve Jobs' decision-fatigue concept; knowing what I am doing on a given day (at least high-level) makes it easier to avoid skipping runs or workouts because I don't have capacity to decide what to do.

## What Am I Reading

_Think Like an Anthropologist_ by Matthew Engelke, and _The Divine Comedy_ by Dante Alighieri (Robin Kirkpatrick translation) 

_Think Like an Anthropologist_ was a Secret Santa gift that I received about eight years ago, and never got around to reading. I find these sorts of books very interesting, but not always easy to get into. Anthropology isn't a field I have any experience with, so I feel like I'm learning quite a lot even at this surface-level book. _The Divine Comedy_ is likewise something I've wanted to read for years now; I don't have any experience of medieval Italian literature though, so it's slow going, but equally I find it very engaging to switch gears into something like this.
